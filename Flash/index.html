BlockBoy Flasher

Your BlockBoy is recognised automatically, so it cannot be given the wrong firmware.


                

                    
                    
                


                

                    

                        
Instructions

                        
    
                            
  • Connect with a USB-A to USB-C cable
    • 
                            
  • Turn the BlockBoy on
    • 
                            
  • Click "Connect device" and pick the port in the popup
    • 
                            
  • Your device is detected automatically
    • 
                            
  • Confirm, then wait ~1-2 minutes
    • 
                            
  • Flashing a second time? Unplug the cable and plug it back in first, then connect straight away
    • 
                            
  • After this, your BlockBoy updates itself from the menu, no cable needed
    • 
                        

                    

                    

                        
Important

                        
The firmware is overwritten. ROMs and saves on the microSD card are not affected.

                        
USB-C to USB-C cables are not supported. Use a USB-A to USB-C cable.

                    

                

            


            

                Tools
                

                    
Only needed for testing or troubleshooting. A normal install does not need this.

                    

                        
                        
                    

                    
Recovery skips the confirm step and re-flashes a stuck or restarting device the moment it is caught, so you do not need to be quick. It uses the firmware selected above and still verifies the hardware first.
